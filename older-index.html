What to do next

Take a photo the model can read

A sharp, well-lit photo of a single leaf gives the most reliable result.

  • Shoot in direct daylight

    Lay the affected leaf flat under natural sunlight, and keep your hand's shadow off it.

  • Frame one leaf

    Get close enough that spots, mildew patches or yellowing fill most of the screen.

  • Wipe your lens first

    Dust blurs fine texture. A clean lens keeps the top and underside of the leaf crisp.

What CropQuery checks for

Each photo is matched against these five outcomes for pumpkin leaves.

Bacterial leaf spot
Small angular spots that start water-soaked, then turn brown, often with a yellow ring.
Downy mildew
Yellow patches shaped by the leaf veins, with grey-purple fuzz underneath in humid weather.
Mosaic disease
Mottled light and dark green patterns, with puckered or curled leaves.
Powdery mildew
White, flour-like patches that spread across the leaf surface.
Healthy
Even green colour with no spots, patches or distortion.

A first check, not a diagnosis. CropQuery gives a preliminary assessment to help you catch problems early. Confirm with a local agricultural advisor before applying any treatment.
